We presented AuralGuard, a multi-view one-class detection framework for AI-generated speech that addresses the core challenges of cross-domain generalization and probability calibration. The architecture synergistically couples a frozen WavLM-Large foundation-model encoder with learnable layer-wise attention to an explicit vocoder-artifact branch via gated cross-attention fusion, optimized under an Orthogonal Centroid Scoring (OCS) one-class objective.

Under a rigorous train-once, evaluate-everywhere protocol across held-out in-domain and diverse out-of-domain benchmarks, our empirical investigation yields definitive answers to the four guiding research questions:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{RQ1 (Generalization):} Foundation-model representations prevent catastrophic collapse, achieving zero-shot EERs of 16.55\% (B5) and 19.19\% (AuralGuard v2) on In-the-Wild, whereas legacy cepstral and raw-waveform baselines degrade to near-chance error rates (38--50\% EER).
    \item \textbf{RQ2 (Calibration):} Open-set one-class learning achieves superior probability calibration under real-world domain shift: AuralGuard attains an outstanding In-the-Wild ECE of 0.0965 (v1) and 0.1716 (v2), vastly surpassing single-view SSL (0.2320) and legacy models ($>$0.55), fulfilling forensic admissibility standards.
    \item \textbf{RQ3 (Multi-View Synergy):} Fusing semantic context with physical phase dynamics (LFCC, modified group delay, CQT phase) via gated cross-attention attains the top performance on multi-vocoder WaveFake (45.19\% EER), overcoming vocoder phase vulnerabilities.
    \item \textbf{RQ4 (Operational Feasibility):} Freezing the 315M backbone and training only 4.49M adapter parameters yields an RTF of 0.058 on GPU and 0.412 on CPU, enabling high-throughput biometric security on commodity hardware.
\end{itemize}

Future work will expand training across emerging neural vocoders and flow-matching synthesizers, extend one-class multi-view scoring to localized temporal spoofing and partial forgery detection, and distill the foundation-model front-end into lightweight streaming architectures for on-device deployment. All source code, configuration files, trained weights, and evaluation manifests are openly released at GitHub (\url{https://github.com/MIHMahmudEli/auralguardv2}) and Hugging Face (\url{https://huggingface.co/MoshinAli/auralguard-checkpoints}) to advance verifiable, calibrated synthetic speech forensics.
