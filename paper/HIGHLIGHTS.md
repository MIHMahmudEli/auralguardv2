# Research Highlights

- Multi-view deepfake detector unites WavLM SSL and physical vocoder artifacts.
- Gated cross-attention fuses high-level semantics with low-level phase cues.
- Evaluated on ASVspoof 2019, In-the-Wild, and multi-vocoder WaveFake benchmarks.
- Out-of-domain ECE of 0.0965 ensures legally admissible probability calibration.
- Zero-shot 19.19% EER on In-the-Wild with only 4.5M trainable adapter parameters.
